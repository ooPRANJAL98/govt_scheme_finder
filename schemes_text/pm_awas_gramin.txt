SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana Gramin (PMAY-G)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide pucca houses with basic amenities to all homeless and those living
in dilapidated houses in rural areas by 2024.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Permanent resident of rural area
- Homeless or living in a kutcha/dilapidated house with zero, one or two rooms
- Name must appear in SECC 2011 data (Socio Economic Caste Census)
- Priority given to SC/ST families
- Priority given to families with no adult male member aged 16-59
- Widows or next of kin of defence personnel killed in action
- Disabled persons
- Families living below poverty line (BPL)

NOT ELIGIBLE:
- Families already owning a pucca house
- Families with motorized vehicles (car, tractor)
- Government employees
- Income tax paying families
- Families with monthly income above Rs. 15,000

BENEFITS:
- Rs. 1.20 lakh financial assistance in plain areas
- Rs. 1.30 lakh financial assistance in hilly/difficult areas including Himachal Pradesh
- Additional Rs. 12,000 for construction of toilets under Swachh Bharat Mission
- Rs. 90 days of unskilled labour wages under MGNREGA for construction
- Total assistance can reach up to Rs. 1.50 lakh in hilly states
- Money transferred directly to bank account in installments
- House must be built within 12-18 months

FEATURES OF HOUSE:
- Minimum area of 25 square meters
- Must have a kitchen, separate toilet, proper ventilation
- Beneficiary chooses their own house design
- Construction done by beneficiary themselves or with local mason

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Check if your name is in SECC 2011 list at pmayg.nic.in
Step 2 - Visit Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office
Step 3 - Fill application form with Aadhaar, bank passbook, land documents
Step 4 - Gram Sabha verifies and approves eligible beneficiaries
Step 5 - After approval, first installment released to bank account
Step 6 - Subsequent installments released after each construction stage

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook
- Land ownership documents or NOC from landowner
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST)
- Disability certificate (if applicable)
- Job card for MGNREGA wages

HIMACHAL PRADESH SPECIFIC:
- HP receives higher assistance of Rs. 1.30 lakh due to hilly terrain
- HP Mukhyamantri Awas Yojana covers families not in SECC list
- Contact: District Rural Development Agency (DRDA) in each HP district

HELPLINE: 1800-11-6446 (toll free)
