SCHEME NAME: HP Sahara Yojana (Himachal Pradesh)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to patients suffering from serious and
chronic diseases in Himachal Pradesh who cannot afford treatment costs.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Must be a permanent resident and domicile of Himachal Pradesh
- Must be suffering from one of the covered serious diseases
- Annual family income must be below Rs. 4 lakh
- Patient must be registered at a government hospital in HP
- Aadhaar card required

DISEASES COVERED:
- Cancer (all types)
- Parkinson's disease
- Paralysis
- Muscular Dystrophy
- Thalassemia
- Hemophilia
- Renal failure (kidney failure requiring dialysis)
- Liver failure
- Cerebral Palsy
- Spinal Muscular Atrophy
- Multiple Sclerosis
- Chronic Kidney Disease

BENEFITS:
- Rs. 2,000 per month financial assistance to patient
- Amount transferred directly to patient's bank account every month
- Assistance continues as long as patient is suffering from the disease
- Assistance available even during ongoing treatment

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Get a diagnosis certificate from a government hospital doctor in HP
Step 2 - Visit nearest Lok Mitra Kendra or apply through HPSBYS portal
Step 3 - Fill HP Sahara Yojana application form
Step 4 - Submit form with all required documents to Chief Medical Officer (CMO)
Step 5 - CMO verifies and approves application
Step 6 - Monthly assistance starts within 30 days of approval

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card of patient
- Aadhaar card of head of family
- HP domicile certificate
- Income certificate from tehsildar (below Rs. 4 lakh)
- Medical certificate from government hospital confirming diagnosis
- Bank account passbook (patient's own or family member's account)
- Passport size photographs
- Ration card

WHERE TO APPLY:
- Chief Medical Officer (CMO) office in your district
- Lok Mitra Kendra in your area
- Online: hpsbys.in
- District Welfare Office

CONTACT:
- HP Social Security and Welfare Department
- District CMO offices across HP
- Helpline: 0177-2629802
- Website: hpsbys.in

STATE: Himachal Pradesh only
CATEGORY: Health, Disability, Social welfare
