SCHEME NAME: National Scholarship Portal Schemes (NSP) - Pre and Post Matric

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to meritorious students from SC, ST, OBC,
minority and economically weaker sections to support their education.

ELIGIBILITY - PRE MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP (Class 9 and 10):
- Student must be studying in Class 9 or Class 10
- Must belong to SC, ST, OBC or Minority community
- Family annual income must be below Rs. 2.5 lakh
- Must have secured at least 55% marks in previous class
- Must be a regular student in a government or recognized school

ELIGIBILITY - POST MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP (Class 11 onwards):
- Student must be studying in Class 11, 12 or any graduate/postgraduate course
- Must belong to SC, ST, OBC or Minority community
- Family annual income must be below Rs. 2.5 lakh (SC/ST) or Rs. 1 lakh (OBC)
- Must have secured at least 50% marks in previous exam
- Must be enrolled in a government or recognized institution

BENEFITS - PRE MATRIC:
- Day scholars: Rs. 150 per month (Class 9-10)
- Hostellers: Rs. 350 per month (Class 9-10)
- Additional allowance for books and stationery: Rs. 750 per year
- Ad hoc grant: Rs. 1,000 per year for SC/ST students

BENEFITS - POST MATRIC:
- For Class 11-12: Rs. 380 per month (day scholar), Rs. 880 per month (hosteller)
- For Graduation: Rs. 570 per month (day scholar), Rs. 1,200 per month (hosteller)
- For Post Graduation: Rs. 820 per month (day scholar), Rs. 1,500 per month (hosteller)
- Full reimbursement of tuition fees for SC students in state government colleges
- Book bank facility for SC/ST students in colleges

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Register on National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in
Step 2 - Fill application form with personal and academic details
Step 3 - Upload required documents
Step 4 - Submit application before deadline (usually September-October)
Step 5 - Institute verifies and forwards to state education department
Step 6 - Scholarship credited directly to student bank account (DBT)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card
- Caste/Community certificate
- Family income certificate from tehsildar or SDM
- Last exam marksheet
- Current year fee receipt from institution
- Bank account passbook (student's own account)
- Bonafide student certificate from institution
- Domicile certificate

HIMACHAL PRADESH SPECIFIC SCHOLARSHIPS:
- HP Post Matric Scholarship for SC students: additional state top-up
- HP Merit Scholarship: Rs. 10,000 per year for top students in HP Board exams
- Medha Protsahan Yojana: free coaching for competitive exams
- Swami Vivekanand Utkrisht Chatravritti: Rs. 10,000 per year for higher studies
- Contact: HP Welfare Department or District Welfare Officer

IMPORTANT DEADLINES:
- Fresh applications: usually August to October every year
- Renewal applications: usually October to November every year
