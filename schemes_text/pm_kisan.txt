SCHEME NAME: PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

OBJECTIVE:
To provide income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Small and marginal farmers with cultivable land up to 2 hectares
- Indian citizen
- Must be actively engaged in farming
- Valid Aadhaar card required
- Bank account linked to Aadhaar

NOT ELIGIBLE:
- Institutional landholders
- Farmer families holding constitutional posts
- Government employees (current/retired)
- Income tax payers
- Professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers

BENEFITS:
- Rs. 6,000 per year paid in 3 installments of Rs. 2,000 each
- Directly transferred to bank account (DBT)
- Every 4 months: April-July, August-November, December-March

HOW TO APPLY:
- Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
- Apply online at pmkisan.gov.in
- Contact local Patwari or Agriculture Officer
- Documents needed: Aadhaar card, land records (Khasra/Khatauni), bank passbook

STATE: All India
CATEGORY: Agriculture
