SCHEME NAME: Dr. Y.S. Parmar Horticulture and Forestry University Schemes and
HP Horticulture Development schemes

OBJECTIVE:
To promote horticulture (apple, pear, plum, cherry, mango, citrus, vegetables)
in Himachal Pradesh by providing subsidies, technical support and market access
to fruit growers and vegetable farmers.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Permanent resident and domicile of Himachal Pradesh
- Must own or lease agricultural or horticultural land in HP
- Registered farmer in HP agriculture or horticulture department
- Valid HP Kisan Card or land records (Khasra/Khatauni)
- Aadhaar card linked to bank account

MAJOR SCHEMES UNDER HP HORTICULTURE:

1. Anti Hail Network Scheme:
- 80% subsidy for SC/ST/BPL farmers on anti-hail nets
- 50% subsidy for general category farmers
- Protects apple and fruit orchards from hail damage
- Apply at: local Horticulture Development Officer (HDO)

2. Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation Subsidy:
- 90% subsidy for small and marginal farmers (land up to 2 hectares)
- 70% subsidy for other farmers
- Available for apple, vegetable, and fruit orchards
- Apply through: Department of Horticulture HP

3. Orchard Development Scheme:
- Rs. 30,000 per hectare subsidy for new apple orchard planting
- Rs. 20,000 per hectare for other fruit orchards
- Free or subsidized saplings from HP Horticulture Nurseries
- Technical guidance from HDO

4. Cold Storage and Pack House Subsidy:
- 35% subsidy on construction of cold storage
- 50% subsidy for SC/ST farmers on pack house construction
- Helps farmers store produce and reduce post-harvest losses

5. Vermi Compost and Organic Farming:
- Rs. 5,000 subsidy per unit for vermi compost pit
- Free organic farming training at KVK centers
- Premium price support for certified organic produce

6. Apple Marketing Support:
- HPMC (HP Horticulture Produce Marketing Corporation) buys apples at MSP
- Minimum Support Price declared each year for apple
- Carton and packaging subsidy for small farmers

BENEFITS SUMMARY:
- Anti-hail net: 50-80% subsidy
- Drip irrigation: 70-90% subsidy
- New orchard: Rs. 20,000-30,000 per hectare
- Power sprayer: 50% subsidy
- Water harvesting tank: Rs. 15,000 per tank subsidy
- Tractor: 25-50% subsidy for small farmers
- Beekeeping boxes for pollination: 50% subsidy

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Visit nearest Horticulture Development Officer (HDO) office in your block
Step 2 - Or visit District Horticulture Officer (DHO) in your district HQ
Step 3 - Fill application form for specific scheme you need
Step 4 - Submit with land records, Aadhaar, bank passbook
Step 5 - HDO inspects land and verifies eligibility
Step 6 - Subsidy released directly to bank account (DBT)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card
- HP domicile certificate
- Land records (Khasra/Khatauni/Jamabandi)
- Bank account passbook
- HP Kisan Card (if available)
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST higher subsidy)
- Photographs of land or existing orchard
