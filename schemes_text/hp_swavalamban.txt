SCHEME NAME: Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana (Himachal Pradesh)

OBJECTIVE:
To promote self employment and entrepreneurship among youth of Himachal Pradesh
by providing capital subsidy on loans taken for setting up new industrial or
service enterprises.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Permanent resident and domicile of Himachal Pradesh
- Age between 18 and 45 years
- Educational qualification: minimum 8th pass
- For women and specially abled persons: age limit relaxed to 50 years
- Should not be a government employee
- Should not be a defaulter of any bank or financial institution
- New enterprise or business (not expansion of existing one for first time)

ELIGIBLE BUSINESS SECTORS:
Manufacturing:
- Food processing units (bakery, pickle, jam, juice)
- Handicraft and handloom units
- Furniture and carpentry workshops
- Electrical and electronic repair shops
- Automobile repair and service centers
- Garment and tailoring units

Service sector:
- Beauty parlours and salons
- Computer and IT related services
- Photography and videography studios
- Dry cleaning and laundry
- Catering and food service
- Tourism and hospitality related

Trade and retail:
- General stores and provision shops
- Medical stores (with required license)
- Stationery and bookshops
- Hardware and plumbing stores

Agriculture allied:
- Mushroom cultivation
- Bee keeping (apiculture)
- Floriculture and nursery
- Dairy units and poultry

BENEFITS:
- 25% capital subsidy on project cost for general category
- 30% capital subsidy for women, SC, ST, OBC, differently abled, ex-servicemen
- Maximum subsidy: Rs. 7 lakh for projects up to Rs. 40 lakh
- Loan available from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 40 lakh
- Subsidy directly credited to loan account reducing EMI burden
- Interest subsidy also available for first 3 years
- No collateral required for loans up to Rs. 10 lakh (combined with MUDRA)

EXAMPLE CALCULATION:
- Project cost: Rs. 5 lakh
- Bank loan: Rs. 4.5 lakh (90%)
- Own contribution: Rs. 50,000 (10%)
- Subsidy at 25%: Rs. 1.25 lakh credited back to loan
- Effective loan to repay: Rs. 3.25 lakh only

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Prepare a business plan or project report
Step 2 - Apply online at himachal.nic.in/swavalamban or visit District Industries Centre (DIC)
Step 3 - Submit application with all documents to DIC in your district
Step 4 - Application reviewed by District Level Committee
Step 5 - Bank loan sanctioned and disbursed
Step 6 - Subsidy released directly to loan account after 6 months of operation

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card and PAN card
- HP domicile certificate
- Age proof (birth certificate or school certificate)
- Educational qualification certificate
- Project report or business plan
- Land or premises documents (ownership or rent agreement)
- Bank account details
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST/OBC)
- Disability certificate (if applicable)
