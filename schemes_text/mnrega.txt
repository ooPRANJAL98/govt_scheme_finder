SCHEME NAME: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year
to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Must be a resident of rural area
- Adult member (18 years or above) of the household
- Willing to do unskilled manual work
- Must have a Job Card issued by local Gram Panchayat
- Valid Aadhaar card required
- Bank or post office account for wage payment

NOT ELIGIBLE:
- Urban area residents
- People seeking skilled or semi-skilled work only
- Households without a Job Card

BENEFITS:
- Minimum 100 days of wage employment per household per year
- Wages paid as per state-notified MGNREGA wage rate
- In Himachal Pradesh: approx Rs. 240-280 per day
- Wages paid directly to bank/post office account within 15 days
- If work not provided within 15 days of application, unemployment allowance is paid
- Work provided within 5 km of residence
- If work site is beyond 5 km, 10% extra wages paid for travel

TYPES OF WORK PROVIDED:
- Water conservation and harvesting
- Drought proofing including afforestation
- Irrigation canals and channels
- Renovation of traditional water bodies
- Land development
- Flood control and protection
- Rural connectivity (roads)
- Agriculture and allied works

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Get a Job Card from Gram Panchayat (free of cost)
         Documents needed: Aadhaar card, passport photo, residence proof
Step 2 - Submit written application to Gram Panchayat requesting work
Step 3 - Work will be allotted within 15 days of application
Step 4 - Register at nrega.nic.in or visit local Block Development Office

IMPORTANT RIGHTS:
- You have a LEGAL RIGHT to demand work
- If work is not given in 15 days, you are entitled to unemployment allowance
- Wages must be paid within 15 days of work completion
- At least one-third of workers must be women

STATE: All India (especially relevant for rural Himachal Pradesh)
CATEGORY: Employment, Rural Development
