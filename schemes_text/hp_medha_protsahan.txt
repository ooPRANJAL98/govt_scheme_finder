SCHEME NAME: Medha Protsahan Yojana (Himachal Pradesh)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide free coaching and financial assistance to meritorious students
from economically weaker sections of Himachal Pradesh for competitive
entrance examinations and other professional courses.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Must be a permanent resident of Himachal Pradesh
- Must have passed Class 12 from an HP school or be studying in HP college
- Family annual income must be below Rs. 2.5 lakh
- Must belong to any of these categories: General, SC, ST, OBC, IRDP, BPL
- Minimum marks required: 65% for General category, 50% for SC/ST/OBC

COMPETITIVE EXAMS COVERED:
- JEE Main and Advanced (Engineering entrance)
- NEET (Medical entrance)
- CLAT (Law entrance)
- UPSC Civil Services Examination
- HP Administrative Services (HAS)
- HP Police Services
- Banking exams (IBPS, SBI)
- SSC (Staff Selection Commission) exams
- NDA (National Defence Academy)
- CA Foundation

BENEFITS:
- Rs. 1 lakh financial assistance per year for outstation coaching
- Rs. 75,000 per year for coaching within Himachal Pradesh
- Coaching fee paid directly to empanelled coaching institutes
- Assistance available for maximum 2 years per student
- Travel allowance for students going for coaching outside HP
- Priority given to girl students, SC/ST students and BPL families

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Visit hp.gov.in/medhaprotsahan or Higher Education Department HP
Step 2 - Fill online application form during application window (usually April-June)
Step 3 - Upload Class 10, Class 12 marksheets and income certificate
Step 4 - Submit application before deadline
Step 5 - Selection based on merit and interview
Step 6 - Selected students get coaching fee paid directly to institute

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card
- Class 10 marksheet and certificate
- Class 12 marksheet (or appearing certificate)
- Family income certificate from tehsildar
- HP domicile certificate
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST/OBC)
- BPL/IRDP certificate (if applicable)
- Bank account details
- Passport size photographs

ADDITIONAL BENEFITS FOR SELECTED STUDENTS:
- Mentoring by senior IAS/IPS officers of HP cadre
- Study material provided
- Online mock tests and practice papers
- Career counselling sessions
