SCHEME NAME: Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

OBJECTIVE:
To secure the financial future of the girl child in India by encouraging
parents to save for their daughter's education and marriage expenses.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Account can be opened for a girl child below 10 years of age
- Parents or legal guardian can open the account
- Maximum two accounts per family (one per girl child)
- Exception: twins or triplets - three accounts allowed
- Girl child must be an Indian citizen and resident

BENEFITS:
- Currently one of the highest interest rates among government savings schemes
- Interest rate: 8.2% per annum (revised quarterly by government)
- Full tax exemption under Section 80C on deposits up to Rs. 1.5 lakh per year
- Interest earned is completely tax free
- Maturity amount is completely tax free
- Account matures when girl turns 21 years old
- Partial withdrawal of 50% allowed after girl turns 18 (for education)

DEPOSIT RULES:
- Minimum deposit: Rs. 250 per year
- Maximum deposit: Rs. 1.5 lakh per year
- Deposits can be made for first 15 years from account opening
- Account continues to earn interest for remaining 6 years without deposits
- Deposits can be made in lump sum or multiple installments

EXAMPLE CALCULATION:
- If Rs. 12,000 deposited per year (Rs. 1,000 per month) for 15 years
- Total deposited: Rs. 1,80,000
- At 8.2% interest, maturity amount at 21 years: approximately Rs. 5,40,000

HOW TO OPEN ACCOUNT:
Step 1 - Visit nearest Post Office or authorized bank (SBI, PNB, HDFC, etc.)
Step 2 - Fill Sukanya Samriddhi Account opening form
Step 3 - Submit documents and initial deposit (minimum Rs. 250)
Step 4 - Passbook issued on the same day
Step 5 - Subsequent deposits can be made at any post office or bank branch
Step 6 - Account can also be operated online through internet banking

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Birth certificate of girl child
- Aadhaar card of girl child
- Aadhaar card of parent or guardian
- Address proof of parent or guardian
- Photograph of parent or guardian
- Initial deposit amount (minimum Rs. 250)

WITHDRAWAL RULES:
- Partial withdrawal: 50% of balance after girl turns 18, for education only
- Full withdrawal: after girl turns 21 (account maturity)
- Premature closure: allowed only for marriage after girl turns 18
- Premature closure also allowed in case of death or serious illness

WHERE TO OPEN:
- Any Post Office in India
- Authorized banks: SBI, Bank of Baroda, PNB, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Canara Bank
- In HP: any post office or bank branch accepts this account
