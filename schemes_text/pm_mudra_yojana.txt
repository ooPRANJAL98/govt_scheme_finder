SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide affordable loans to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro
enterprises for starting or expanding their business without collateral.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Indian citizen above 18 years of age
- Running or planning to start a small business
- Business should be non-agricultural (trading, manufacturing, services)
- No existing loan defaults
- Valid Aadhaar and PAN card
- Applicable for shopkeepers, vendors, artisans, small manufacturers

TYPES OF MUDRA LOANS:
1. SHISHU - Loans up to Rs. 50,000 (for new startups and very small businesses)
2. KISHOR - Loans from Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 5 lakh (for established businesses)
3. TARUN - Loans from Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh (for expanding businesses)

ELIGIBLE BUSINESS TYPES:
- Street vendors and shopkeepers
- Small manufacturing units (food processing, garments, leather)
- Repair shops (mobile, electronics, vehicles)
- Salon and beauty parlours
- Tailoring and weaving units
- Transport operators (auto, taxi, e-rickshaw)
- Food stalls and small restaurants
- Handicraft and artisan units
- Dairy and poultry (non-crop activities)

BENEFITS:
- Loans without any collateral or guarantee
- Lower interest rates than private moneylenders
- No processing fee for Shishu loans
- Flexible repayment terms
- MUDRA RuPay debit card for easy withdrawal
- Working capital and term loan both available
- Women entrepreneurs get 0.25% lower interest rate

INTEREST RATES:
- Varies by bank, typically 8% to 12% per annum
- Shishu loans: around 8-10%
- Kishor and Tarun: around 10-12%
- Repayment period: 3 to 5 years

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Decide loan amount needed and which category (Shishu/Kishor/Tarun)
Step 2 - Prepare business plan or description of your business
Step 3 - Visit nearest bank, NBFC, MFI or apply online at mudra.org.in
Step 4 - Submit application with required documents
Step 5 - Bank verifies and disburses loan within 7-14 working days

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card and PAN card
- Passport size photographs
- Business address proof
- Quotation for machinery or equipment (if purchasing)
- Bank account statement of last 6 months
- Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC for priority)
- For Kishor and Tarun: balance sheet and income proof

HIMACHAL PRADESH SPECIFIC:
- HP Mukhyamantri Swavalamban Yojana gives additional subsidy on top of MUDRA
- Young entrepreneurs in HP aged 18-45 can get 25% capital subsidy
- Contact: HP State Cooperative Bank or any nationalized bank branch
