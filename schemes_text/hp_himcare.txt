SCHEME NAME: HIMCARE - Himachal Pradesh Health Care Scheme

OBJECTIVE:
To provide health insurance coverage to families in Himachal Pradesh who are
not covered under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme. Ensures no HP resident is
left without health protection.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Permanent resident and domicile of Himachal Pradesh
- Must NOT be already covered under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
- Any HP resident can enroll by paying a small annual premium
- Special categories with FREE enrollment:
  - MNREGA workers with job card
  - Street vendors registered under PM SVANidhi
  - Registered construction workers
  - Anganwadi workers and helpers
  - ASHA workers
  - Mid-day meal workers
  - Daily wage government employees

PREMIUM AMOUNT:
- BPL families: Rs. 0 (completely free)
- MNREGA job card holders: Rs. 0 (completely free)
- Annual income below Rs. 1.5 lakh: Rs. 365 per year (Rs. 1 per day)
- Annual income Rs. 1.5 lakh to Rs. 3 lakh: Rs. 730 per year
- Annual income above Rs. 3 lakh: Rs. 1,000 per year

BENEFITS:
- Health coverage of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year
- Covers entire family with no limit on family size
- Cashless treatment at all empanelled government and private hospitals in HP
- Covers pre-existing diseases from day one of enrollment
- Covers 1,700 plus medical procedures
- 3 days pre-hospitalization expenses covered
- 15 days post-hospitalization expenses covered
- No age limit for any family member
- Covers medicines, diagnostics, ICU, surgery, specialist fees

MAJOR TREATMENTS COVERED:
- Cancer treatment and chemotherapy
- Heart bypass surgery and angioplasty
- Kidney dialysis and transplant
- Brain surgery and neurological treatments
- Knee and hip replacement
- Eye surgeries
- Maternity and newborn care
- Covid-19 and other infectious diseases

HOW TO ENROLL:
Step 1 - Visit nearest Lok Mitra Kendra or Common Service Centre (CSC) in HP
Step 2 - Carry Aadhaar card and HP domicile certificate
Step 3 - Fill HIMCARE enrollment form
Step 4 - Pay applicable premium (or enroll free if eligible)
Step 5 - HIMCARE card issued on the spot
Step 6 - Show card at any empanelled hospital for cashless treatment

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card of all family members
- HP domicile certificate or HP voter ID
- Income certificate (for premium calculation)
- BPL/MNREGA card (if applicable for free enrollment)
- Ration card
- Passport size photographs

EMPANELLED HOSPITALS IN HP:
- All government hospitals across HP (IGMC Shimla, Dr RPGMC Tanda, etc.)
- Zonal hospitals in each district
- Selected private hospitals across HP

CONTACT:
- HP State Benefit and You Scheme (HPSBYS) office
- Toll free helpline: 1800-180-8900
- Website: hpsbys.in
- District hospitals across HP

STATE: Himachal Pradesh only
