SCHEME NAME: Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide health protection cover to poor and vulnerable families for
secondary and tertiary hospitalization. Largest government-funded health
insurance scheme in the world.

ELIGIBILITY:
Based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data. Eligible if any of
the following conditions apply to your household:

RURAL ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS (any one is enough):
- Households with only one room and kuccha walls and roof
- Households with no adult member between 16 and 59 years
- Female-headed household with no adult male member between 16 and 59 years
- Household with disabled member and no able-bodied adult member
- SC/ST households
- Landless households earning major income from manual casual labour
- Households living in primitive tribal groups
- Legally released bonded labourers
- Families in BPL (Below Poverty Line) category

URBAN ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS (occupational categories):
- Ragpicker, beggar, domestic worker, street vendor
- Construction worker, plumber, mason, painter, welder
- Washer-man, watchman, sweeper, sanitation worker
- Home-based worker, artisan, handicraft worker
- Driver, conductor, helper in transport
- Shop worker, assistant, peon, delivery boy
- Electrician, mechanic, assembler, repair worker

NOT ELIGIBLE:
- Families owning motorized vehicle (2/3/4 wheeler or fishing boat)
- Families owning mechanized agricultural equipment
- Government employees
- Families with monthly income above Rs. 10,000
- People paying income tax
- Families with refrigerator or landline phone

BENEFITS:
- Health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year
- Covers all members of the family (no limit on family size)
- Covers pre-existing diseases from day one
- Cashless and paperless treatment at empanelled hospitals
- Covers 3 days pre-hospitalization and 15 days post-hospitalization expenses
- Covers over 1,500 medical procedures and treatments
- No age limit for coverage
- Covers medicines, diagnostics, ICU charges, surgeon fees

DISEASES AND TREATMENTS COVERED:
- Cancer treatments including chemotherapy
- Heart surgeries (bypass, valve replacement)
- Kidney dialysis and transplant
- Orthopaedic surgeries (knee/hip replacement)
- Neurological conditions and brain surgery
- Eye surgeries including cataract
- Maternal and child health procedures
- Covid-19 treatment

HOW TO CHECK ELIGIBILITY:
- Visit pmjay.gov.in and enter mobile number or ration card number
- Call helpline: 14555 or 1800-111-565 (toll free)
- Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
- Visit Ayushman Mitra at any empanelled government hospital

HOW TO GET AYUSHMAN CARD:
Step 1 - Check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in or call 14555
Step 2 - Visit nearest CSC or government hospital with Aadhaar card
Step 3 - Biometric verification done on the spot
Step 4 - Ayushman Bharat card issued immediately (free of cost)
Step 5 - Show card at any empanelled hospital for cashless treatment

HIMACHAL PRADESH SPECIFIC:
- HP has its own extension called HIMCARE for those not covered under PM-JAY
- HIMCARE covers families not in SECC list with Rs. 5 lakh cover at Rs. 365/year premium
- For HP HIMCARE: visit hpsbys.in or nearest HPSBYS office

IMPORTANT NUMBERS:

STATE: All India
