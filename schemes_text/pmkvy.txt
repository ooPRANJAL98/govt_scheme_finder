SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

OBJECTIVE:
To enable a large number of Indian youth to take up skill training and become
employable and earn their livelihood. Provides free short-term skill training
with government certification recognized by industries.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Indian citizen between 15 and 45 years of age
- School or college dropout or currently unemployed
- Class 10 pass not required for most courses
- Aadhaar card required
- Bank account required for stipend
- Priority given to rural youth, women, SC/ST, differently abled persons

COURSES AVAILABLE (300+ courses across sectors):
- IT and computers (data entry, mobile repair, hardware)
- Construction (mason, plumber, electrician, painter)
- Beauty and wellness (salon work, spa therapy)
- Healthcare (general duty assistant, home health aide)
- Retail (sales associate, store operations)
- Automotive (two-wheeler repair, driving)
- Agriculture (organic farming, horticulture)
- Hospitality (food and beverage, housekeeping)
- Textile and apparel (sewing machine operator, tailor)
- Electronics (repair technician, LED light repair)
- Banking financial services (accounts executive)

DURATION:
- Short term courses: 2 weeks to 6 months
- Most popular courses: 3 months

BENEFITS:
- Completely FREE training - no fees charged to candidate
- Daily stipend during training: Rs. 100 to Rs. 150 per day
- Free uniform and study material
- National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) certificate on passing
- Certificate recognized by industries across India
- Placement assistance after training completion
- Accident insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh during training period
- Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for those with existing skills

TRAINING CENTERS:
- PMKVY Training Centers (PMTC) across India
- In HP: Skill Development Allowance Society (SDAS) centers
- Industrial Training Institutes (ITI) across HP
- Private training partners empanelled under PMKVY

HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1 - Find nearest PMKVY training center at pmkvyofficial.org
Step 2 - Walk in directly to training center or register online
Step 3 - Choose your preferred course from available options
Step 4 - Submit Aadhaar card and bank account details
Step 5 - Training starts within 1-2 weeks
Step 6 - Appear for assessment exam at end of course
Step 7 - Receive certificate and placement assistance

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account passbook
- Passport size photographs
- Educational certificate (if available - not mandatory for all courses)
- Caste certificate (optional - for priority)

HIMACHAL PRADESH SPECIFIC:
- HP Kaushal Vikas Nigam runs additional state skill programs
- Chief Minister Yuva Swavalamban Yojana gives additional benefits in HP
- HP ITIs offer PMKVY courses in all 12 districts
- Tribal areas of Kinnaur, Lahaul-Spiti get special mobile training units
- Contact: HP Kaushal Vikas Nigam, Shimla or nearest ITI

AFTER CERTIFICATION:
- Job fairs organized regularly at district level
- Tie-ups with local employers and industries
- Self employment support through MUDRA loan referrals
- Entrepreneurship development programs for interested candidates
